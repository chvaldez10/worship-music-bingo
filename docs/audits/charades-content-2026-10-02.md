A read-only audit of the connected Lovable Cloud database. No database rows or gameplay were changed.

## How difficulty was judged

These are editorial estimates for mixed church-camp teams, not results from participant playtesting. Familiarity varies by age and congregation. Each prompt was judged for a concrete action that an actor can start quickly, a recognizable subject, a distinguishable answer, and suitability for one silent actor. Speaking, singing, spelling, and lip-syncing lyrics are not needed for an easy prompt.

- **Easy:** obvious physical cues and a familiar subject, with reasonable equivalent answers accepted.
- **Medium:** actable, but context, exact names, abstract words, or similar prompts can make guessing slower.
- **Hard:** depends on less familiar Bible details or an abstract song title with weak visual cues.

## Audit of the proposed additions

The previous proposed batch should not be described as all easy. The following eight Bible additions are poor choices for an easy default round:

- Sarah laughing at the promise of a baby: the laughing action does not identify Sarah or the promise.
- Joshua and Caleb exploring the promised land: generic exploring cannot identify the two characters.
- Gideon checking the fleece: niche story detail and unclear physical action.
- Gideon’s army breaking jars and blowing trumpets: two actions, a group, and less familiar story context.
- Elijah being fed by ravens: actor must communicate a prophet, birds, and being fed.
- Elisha making an axe head float: unusual story detail; easily confused with generic woodcutting.
- Esther approaching the king: generic royal encounter gives weak cues to Esther.
- Nehemiah rebuilding the city wall: building a wall is clear, but identifying Nehemiah is difficult.

Several other proposed Bible entries are medium rather than easy: Abraham’s journey, Miriam dancing, manna, Rahab hiding spies, Samuel hearing God, David playing for Saul, Elijah calling down fire, Lazarus rising, the Last Supper, and Paul’s prison or shipwreck stories. Accept an accurate story description instead of requiring every character name.

The proposed church activities have concrete actions and are generally suitable. Memorizing a Bible verse is weaker than reading the Bible; recording a sermon can look like filming anything. New musical actions overlap existing worship prompts. Donation packing and clothing sorting also overlap. Spread these across turns rather than drawing several similar prompts consecutively.

## Recommended camp setup

Use Bible events and church activities for the easiest rounds, accept equivalent descriptions, and keep Pass available without a point penalty. Worship song Charades is a challenge category unless its song subset is curated for physical cues. Do not remove songs from the shared bank to fix Charades: Bingo and Singing Bee still need them.

Clarify vague activity prompts before expanding the bank. Prioritize recognizable actions rather than reaching a target count. A host dry run with a few people from this camp is the best way to calibrate difficulty; this audit does not claim participant-tested recognition rates.

The app currently loads Charades content from src/data/game-prompts.ts and worship titles from src/data/songs.ts. Database-only edits will not change the game until that content is connected or synchronized.
